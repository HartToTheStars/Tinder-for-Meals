Privacy policy

The short version

We will never ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever ever … EVER sell your data.

Seriously, who does that?

What forks keeps

  • Just browsing (no account): your swipes and keeps stay on your device, in your browser's storage. We don't see them, beyond the anonymous list of recipes you've already swiped that your phone sends so the deck doesn't repeat itself.
  • With an account: your email address, the recipes you swiped left or right, and when. That's the whole list.

Why

  • Your email is only used to send you sign-in links. No newsletters, no "we miss you."
  • Your swipes are used to show your keeps and to nudge your deck toward food you like. That learning runs on your swipes alone and is never shared.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase stores accounts and swipes.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails, and forwards site suggestions from the sources page to us. If you add your email to a suggestion, it's only used to reply to you.
  • here.now hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the fonts and one code library.
  • Recipe photos load straight from the sites that published them (Bon Appétit, Smitten Kitchen, Half Baked Harvest, The Kitchn). Tapping a recipe takes you to their site, where their own policies apply.

None of them get your data to use for themselves, and there are no ads, ad trackers, or analytics cookies here.

Lists, follows and sharing

  • Your list is unlisted. It never shows up in search or suggestions. When you share a recipe from a signed-in account, the person you send it to sees your first name and can follow your keeps. Kelly's list is the only one we feature.
  • Following someone is private too. They don't see who follows them.
  • "Kept by N people" on a card is an anonymous count, and it's only shown once at least 3 people have kept that recipe.
  • Sharing a recipe creates a link to that one recipe. It doesn't reveal anything else you've kept.

Staying signed in

Once you sign in, you stay signed in on that device until you sign out. The session is kept in your browser's storage.

Leaving

Signed in? Tap the account button, then Delete my account. Your email and every swipe are erased right away. Just browsing? Clearing your browser's site data for forks wipes everything.
