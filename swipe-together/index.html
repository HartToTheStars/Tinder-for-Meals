Questions

How does Swipe with someone work?
One person taps Swipe with someone on forkslist.com and sends the link. Everyone who opens it swipes the same 12 dinners on their own phone. forks then shows the dinners everyone kept.
Does everyone need an account?
No. Whoever opens the link can swipe right away. Signing in only matters if you want your keeps saved across devices.
How many people can swipe in one round?
Up to 8 people, which covers couples, roommates and most families.
Where do the recipes come from?
From Bon Appétit, Half Baked Harvest and Smitten Kitchen. Every match links to the original recipe on that site.
What happens to the recipes we pick?
The dinners you swipe right on also land in your own keeps, so you can find them later. A round that sits idle for 2 hours starts fresh next time.
