Questions

Is there an app like Tinder for recipes?
Yes, several. forks is one that runs in a web browser, so it works on any phone or computer with nothing to install. You swipe right on recipes from Bon Appétit, Half Baked Harvest and Smitten Kitchen to keep them and left to pass.
Which recipe swipe app works for couples?
forks, Dinners and WhatDinner all let two people swipe and see matches. forks is free, needs no account for the people you invite, and works for up to 8 people in a round.
Is there a free recipe swipe app without a subscription?
forks is free with no subscription and no ads. Several iPhone swipe apps charge a subscription or a one-time fee for filters.
How is a swipe app different from Mealime or Paprika?
Meal planners and recipe boxes like Mealime, Paprika and AnyList are for organizing recipes you already chose. A swipe app helps you choose: it shows one idea at a time so you can decide fast, alone or together.
