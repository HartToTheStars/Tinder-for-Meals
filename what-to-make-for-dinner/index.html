Questions

How does this pick dinners?
It shuffles 60 recent dinners from Bon Appétit, Half Baked Harvest and Smitten Kitchen every time you open the page. Swipe right on anything that sounds good, left to see the next one.
Can I narrow it down?
Yes. Open a collection below, like 30-minute or vegetarian dinners, or tap Keep swiping to filter by course, diet and time in forks.
My partner and I can never agree. Can we pick together?
Start a round of Swipe with someone. You each swipe the same 12 dinners on your own phone, and forks shows the ones you both kept.
Is it free?
Yes. No app to install and no account needed. Sign in only if you want your keeps on more than one device.
